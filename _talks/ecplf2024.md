At ECPLF 2024, I presented the EnergyTag project, an animal experiment conducted in 2023–2024 involving four pigs (1-9 months old). We developed a model to predict energy expenditure using non-invasive Calera sensor data (ODBA, heat flux, and skin temperature) alongside environmental variables including temperature and humidity. I was responsible for the experimental design and execution, and co-supervised MSc thesis student Yajing Wang together with Ali Youssef.
