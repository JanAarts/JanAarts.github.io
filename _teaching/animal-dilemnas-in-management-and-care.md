Delivered sessions on ethical dilemmas in animal management and care, guiding students in constructing structured arguments and engaging in critical discussion. Dilemmas discussed include: Should a goldfish be kept in a bowl? Was the ban on mink farming in the Netherlands a must? Is it a great idea to use dogs for mine detection?
